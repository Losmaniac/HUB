You inherit a 7-person team and one quarter to ship a real product. Work flows through interlocking streams — design feeds frontend, QA gates release — so raw effort isn't enough. Push too hard and defects pile up. Neglect your people and they burn out and quit. Forget your stakeholders and you're off the project. Every week you get a few focus points. You cannot do everything. Lead.
